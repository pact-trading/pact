Pact gives a business everything it needs to get paid in stablecoins: products, checkout links, an embeddable checkout, invoices, subscriptions, refunds, exports and webhooks. Your customers pay with an email sign-in. They never need a wallet app, a seed phrase or gas.

<Note>
Pact runs on **Whitechain Sepolia** (testnet, chain ID 1874). Test USDT and WBT have no monetary value. Card payments are planned as a second payment method.
</Note>

## Why settle in USDT onchain

1. **A refund is a public record.** You refund, and the customer can check the amount and the original payment on the explorer. There are no chargebacks to dispute.
2. **Subscription limits live in a contract.** The customer signs a price cap and a billing period. A renewal that is too early or too large is refused, and anyone can read the refusal and its reason.
3. **You pay gas for your customers, with limits the chain enforces.** Each merchant has its own gas tank, a daily cap, replay protection and a function allowlist. A card network has nothing like it.
4. **Settlement is fast and cheap.** Whitechain has one-second blocks, and a transfer costs about 0.000105 WBT. Small payments and same-block settlement work, which wires and cards can't do for global customers.
