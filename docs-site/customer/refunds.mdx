title: Your refunds
description: Where to see a refund and how to check it yourself.
---

When a business refunds you, the USDT goes straight back to your Pact account. You don't need to do anything.

## See your refund

For a product checkout, open [pay.pactagent.app/portal](https://pay.pactagent.app/portal) and click **Purchases**. A refunded product order shows **Refunded** or **Partially refunded**, with:

- the amount and the reason the business gave,
- the original payment it belongs to,
- a link to the refund on the Whitechain explorer.

If your account has an email address and refund notifications are enabled, Pact sends an email. Telegram messages are available when the business has configured the Pact bot and you have connected it.

Direct payments, manual orders and payment-link payments do not yet have a Purchases entry in the portal. Keep their hosted paid confirmation or receipt email, and ask the business for the refund transaction hash when needed.

## Check it yourself

The refund is a public record on Whitechain. Click the explorer link to see that the USDT really moved, when, and from which business. You don't have to trust the business or Pact.

## How long it takes

Refunds usually arrive within a few seconds of the business sending them.

## Asking for a refund

Refunds are up to the business. Use the business's support contact to request one; Pact's receipt email comes from a no-reply address. A business can refund for up to 30 days after the payment, unless they set a different window.
