A business sent you a Pact checkout link, or you clicked **Pay** in their app. You'll pay in USDT, a digital dollar. You don't need to install anything.

## Pay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Continue with email">
    Click **Continue with email** and type your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    We email you a 6-digit code. Type it in. That's your account: next time, the same email signs you in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add USDT if you need to">
    If your balance is too low, the checkout shows you how to add USDT. On the testnet, click **Swap 0.1 WBT** to get about 8 test USDT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Pay">
    Check the amount and the business name, then click **Pay** and wait for confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For product checkouts, you'll see a receipt with a Whitechain explorer link. Direct and manual-order hosted pages show a paid confirmation, but may not show a transaction link. If your account has an email address and receipts are enabled, Pact also sends an email.

## Things you never have to do

- Install a wallet app or write down a seed phrase.
- Choose a network.
- Pay a gas fee. The business pays it for you.

## Is it safe?

- Your account is protected by your email. Nobody can pay from it without the code.
- A business can only charge what you approve. For a subscription, you sign a [maximum per period](/customer/subscriptions) that the contract enforces.
- Every payment is public on the explorer, so you can always prove what you paid and to whom.

## Your account

Open [pay.pactagent.app/portal](https://pay.pactagent.app/portal) and sign in with the same email to see product purchases, [subscriptions](/customer/subscriptions) and refunds on those purchases. Direct payments, manual orders and payment-link payments do not yet appear in the portal's **Purchases** list; keep their hosted paid confirmation or receipt email, and ask the business for a transaction hash if needed.
