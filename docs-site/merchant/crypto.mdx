Pact's first payment method is USDT on Whitechain. Card payments are planned as a second method; your products, plans and links will not change when it arrives.

## What the customer does

1. Opens your checkout and clicks **Continue with email**.
2. Enters the code sent to their inbox.
3. Clicks **Pay**.

That's all. They never install a wallet app, write down a seed phrase, pick a network, or hold WBT for gas. See [Pay with email](/customer/pay-with-email) for the customer's view.

## What happens underneath

| The customer sees | What Pact does |
| --- | --- |
| Sign in with email | Privy creates an account (an embedded wallet) tied to that email |
| First payment | The USDT approval is sent for them with EIP-7702, paid from your gas tank. No WBT needed. |
| **Pay** | They sign one message. Pact's relayer sends it through the Router, which charges the USDT and takes the gas back from **your** tank in the same transaction. |
| Receipt | The payment is final in about one second (Whitechain has one-second blocks) and has an explorer link. |

Because you pay the gas, a sponsored payment must be at least **1.00 USDT**, and Pact charges a small USDT fee that covers it. See [Gas tank](/merchant/gas-tank) for what it costs and [Drain protection](/merchant/drain-protection) for why nobody can abuse it.
