Your customers never pay gas. You do, from your own **gas tank**: a balance of WBT held for you in the `GasTank` contract. After each sponsored call, the Router pays the relayer back from your tank, at the gas actually used, in the same transaction. Another merchant's customers can never spend your WBT.

## Fund the tank

1. Get WBT. On the testnet, claim it from the [Whitechain faucet](https://faucet.testnet.whitechain.io) (one drip per GitHub account every 24 hours).
2. Open **Gas tank** and click **Deposit**. Enter an amount (0.2 WBT is plenty to start) and confirm in your wallet.
3. Set a **daily cap**: the most WBT your tank may spend in one day. Calls above it are refused, so a spike can never empty the tank.

You can withdraw unused WBT at any time.

## What it costs

A plain transfer on Whitechain costs about **0.000105 WBT**. A sponsored payment uses a little more because it also runs the Router's checks. In practice 0.2 WBT covers more than a thousand payments.

| Page | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Balance | WBT in your tank |
| Daily cap | Spent today out of your cap |
| Runway | Days left at your average spend over the last 7 days with spending |
| Ledger | Every reimbursement, labelled with the payment it paid for and an explorer link |

When runway drops under **7 days**, Pact queues an email if you entered an address under **Settings → Notifications** and enabled gas-tank alerts. **Overview** shows a coloured runway meter.

## Absorb the gas or pass it on

Under **Gas tank → Policy**, choose:

- **Absorb**: you pay the gas. The customer pays the price you set.
- **Surcharge**: the Pact fee on each payment grows to cover the gas, so the WBT you spend comes back to you in USDT.

Either way the customer still never holds WBT.

## Where the WBT comes from on mainnet

On the testnet you top up from the faucet. On mainnet, Pact will refill your tank automatically: when runway falls under your threshold, it swaps a share of your USDT fee revenue for WBT on Whitechain's DEX. Your payment volume pays for its own gas.

## What isn't paid from your tank

Keeper renewals, x402 settlements and agent setup are paid by Pact's relayer today, not from your tank.
